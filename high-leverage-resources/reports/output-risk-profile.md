# Output Risk Profile

## Output family

Evidence-backed Markdown recommendation with a short learning plan. It is high-trust enough that invented availability, pricing, internal structure, or quality claims would undermine the result.

## Highest risks and controls

| Risk | Control in the skill | Final repair |
|---|---|---|
| A resource does not exist, changed, or is inaccessible | Primary-source verification and checked-access field | Open each primary URL before delivery; remove unavailable items. |
| Price, chapter, duration, or update detail is fabricated or stale | State only verified details; use `price varies` when needed | Delete or qualify every unverified fact. |
| Generic five-item list with no decision logic | Outcome-fit selection rubric and curator's note | Explain why each item earns a distinct role this week. |
| Forced diversity lowers quality | Diversity is a preference, not a quota | Keep a same-format exception only when explicitly justified. |
| “Don't waste time” becomes unsupported dismissal | Replace it with sourced limitation or transparent goal-based deprioritization | Rephrase absolute or unverified warnings. |
| Seven-day plan is padded or introduces hidden material | Time budget and selected-resources-only constraint | Check each day for a concrete outcome and permitted source. |

## Missing evidence policy

If current availability, price, lesson structure, or an independent quality signal cannot be verified, label it `missing evidence` in the working notes, omit the claim from the final output, and select a better-supported alternative when possible.
